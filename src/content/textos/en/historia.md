¡Disfrute! is Montevideo's first açaí shop. We're a Uruguayan brand inspired by the Amazon rainforest, where açaí is born: a superfruit that is winning over the world with its flavor, its healthy properties and its unique way of pairing with all kinds of toppings.

¡Disfrute! is a one-of-a-kind place serving authentic Brazilian açaí, created for a delicious experience in an exclusive, Instagram-worthy space.

**Our mission:** to offer authentic experiences of flavor and well-being, bringing the freshness of the Amazon and the joy of sharing good moments to people of every culture.
